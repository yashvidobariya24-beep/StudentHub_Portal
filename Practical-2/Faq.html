Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the process to create an account on EduCentral?

Navigate to the Sign Up page, submit your basic academic details, and complete the registration form.

2. How can I sign in to my student portal?

Provide your registered email address and account password on the official Login page.

3. Is it possible to modify my personal details?

Yes. Access your Profile section and select the Edit Profile option to update your records.

4. Where do I check my subject-wise class attendance?

Log into your account dashboard and open the Attendance Tracking section.

5. How do I stay updated with upcoming campus events?

Head over to the Campus Events page to browse scheduled competitions, workshops, and fests.

6. What steps should I take if I forget my password?

Use the Forgot Password link on the login page or contact the IT Helpdesk for assistance.

7. How can I get in touch with the support team?

Submit a query on our Contact Us page or write directly to support@educentral.ac.in.
